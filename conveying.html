rollers

Megamine Solution Africa Ltd. supplies a range of industrial rollers and accessories designed to support efficient material handling across demanding operating environments. Our range includes directional rollers for guided material movement, disc return rollers for conveyor belt return systems, impact rollers for absorbing shock and handling heavy loads, and smooth rollers for reducing friction and ensuring consistent material flow. We also supply roller accessories to support system functionality and performance. When selecting rollers, factors such as load capacity, operating conditions, and application requirements are essential to ensuring reliable operation, improved efficiency, and extended equipment service life.

CONVEYOR BELTS

The PRIMA CHEVRONS conveyor belts are designed to handle inclined surfaces, ensuring that materials remain secure during transit. With a unique chevron profile, these belts prevent slippage, making them ideal for conveying bulk materials in elevating and lowering applications. When selecting a chevron belt, consider the angle of incline and the type of material being conveyed to ensure optimal performance. On the other hand, PRIMA PLY belts are constructed with multiple plies of fabric, enhancing their strength and durability for heavy-duty applications. These belts are suitable for a wide range of industries, including mining, agriculture, and manufacturing. Key specifications to note include the tensile strength and width of the belt, which should match the operational requirements for safe and efficient material transport.
