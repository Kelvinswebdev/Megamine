WHO WE ARE

Megamine Solutions Africa Limited

Founded in 2009, Megamine Solution Africa Ltd. provides industrial solutions to the manufacturing, mining, quarrying, and construction industries across Ghana and the wider West African region.

As a growing company, we remain committed to strengthening our capabilities, expanding our product portfolio, and responding to the evolving needs of the industries we serve. Our leadership has played an active role in developing the business, fostering strategic relationships, and broadening our range of products and solutions to meet diverse operational requirements.
